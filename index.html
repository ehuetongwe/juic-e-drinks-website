Flavor Profiles

Discover the unique benefits and characteristics of each signature blend

The Refresher

Green Apple · Cucumber · Lemon · Ginger · Key Lime

A Light, Hydrating Reset

A crisp, refreshing blend made to gently cleanse and rehydrate your body — ideal for first-time cleansers or anyone needing a light reset.

What's Inside & Why It Works:

Green Apple – Naturally sweet and full of fiber, green apples help your digestion, keep your blood sugar steady, and support a healthy gut.
Cucumber – Made mostly of water, cucumbers deeply hydrate your body and give your skin a healthy glow thanks to minerals like potassium.
Lemon & Key Lime – These citrus fruits help your body flush out toxins, support digestion, and bring balance to your system by reducing acidity.
Ginger – A warming root that's known for calming the stomach, boosting immunity, and reducing inflammation. It keeps your digestion moving and your body protected.

The Reboot

Green Apple · Beets · Lemon · Ginger

A Bold Cleanse for Deeper Renewal

This earthy, energizing blend supports deeper detox — perfect for more experienced cleansers or after high-stress periods.

What's Inside & Why It Works:

Beets – Beets are a natural detox hero. They help clean your liver, improve blood flow, and give your body a boost of lasting energy.
Green Apple – Adds a naturally sweet flavor while helping with digestion and supporting a healthy gut thanks to its fiber and antioxidants.
Lemon – Helps your body flush out waste and supports your liver. It also brings a fresh, clean taste that helps your system feel balanced.
Ginger – A powerful root that fights inflammation, supports digestion, and helps your body stay strong against sickness.
